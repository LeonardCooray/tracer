# Prior art: Screenbrush, Lightshot, Presentify (30 Oct 2026)

Web research only (App Store listings, review sites, user reviews). No app was installed or inspected with REA, so UI details and exact behaviour are unverified. Prices vary by region and listing.

## ScreenBrush (Mac, App Store id1233965871)

Menu bar app for drawing over the screen during presentations and meetings. Free core, ScreenBrush Plus upgrade (about USD 4.99 in the US, 7-day trial).

- **Free:** pen over windows and full-screen apps, laser pointer, flashlight (highlights one part of the screen), whiteboard mode (opaque background), snapshots, colour wheel, semi-transparent ink, fading ink, customisable shortcuts, multiple-screen support (claimed), and a phone remote that acts as a Keynote/PowerPoint clicker.
- **Plus:** freeze mode (pauses the screen under the ink), arrow, line, rectangle, ellipse, text, numbered badges, eraser, undo/redo, custom clear-screen behaviour, exportable settings and shortcut presets.
- **What users like:** fading ink for pointing during a talk; keyboard control (Tab toggles between the two most-used tools; the toolbar can be summoned and dismissed by key so nothing distracting shows).
- **What users dislike:** the free tier only has fading lines; it reportedly doesn't work on an external monitor (no fix seen); the toolbar can be out of view when sharing a single window, so you end up memorising shortcuts.

## Lightshot (Mac)

Free screenshot tool, not a live-drawing tool. Hotkey ⇧⌘9 on Mac (customisable).

- **Flow:** press the hotkey, drag a region (or take the full screen), and a small toolbar appears beside the selection.
- **Editing on the capture:** pen, line, arrow, rectangle, circle, highlighter (marker), text, colour picker.
- **Output:** copy, save, upload to prnt.sc for a short share link, search for similar images, or open in the web editor (crop, rotate, layers, filters; PNG/JPG/BMP).
- **What users like:** two-step speed (hotkey, drag), the tools sit right next to the selection, instant share links.
- **What users dislike:** limited capture modes (no scrolling capture). Not from these sources but widely reported: a dated look, and prnt.sc links being public and guessable.

## Presentify (Mac, App Store id1507246666), closest live-drawing competitor

- Menu bar app; draw over any screen with colours, shapes and gradients; undo/redo per screen.
- Works across multiple screens without toggling annotate off and on, and keeps settings per screen.
- Cursor highlight (circle, squircle, rhombus), whiteboard, spotlight, zoom.
- About USD 7 one-time (App Store/Setapp), though one listing describes a Pro subscription.
- Whether clicks pass through the ink to the apps underneath isn't documented in what I found.

## Takeaways for our app

- Fading ink, a spotlight and a cursor highlight are the core pointing tools across all of these.
- Keyboard-first control matters: summon, switch tools and dismiss without a toolbar on screen.
- External monitors are a known weak spot for ScreenBrush and a stated strength for Presentify, so multi-display support should be in from the start.
- Lightshot's best idea is the toolbar anchored next to the thing you're working on, rather than a fixed palette.
- Freeze mode bridges live drawing and screenshots: pause the screen, draw, then capture.
